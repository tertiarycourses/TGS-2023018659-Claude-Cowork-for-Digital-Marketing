## About the course

One business runs through every lab: **Horizon Wealth Planning**, a fictitious Singapore financial-planning firm with a website, a goal of 40 booked chats a month and no marketing team. You build it one, made of AI agents. A Marketing Team Lead and five specialists (Growth Strategist, Content Creator, Creative Designer, Website Designer and Growth Analyst) each get instructions, skills and connectors.

The course treats that team as a system integration problem:

- **Assess and integrate.** Assess compatibility at every interface (files, MCP connectors, OAuth sign-ins and platform APIs) and integrate the components.
- **Test and troubleshoot.** Test each integration through real campaign work and troubleshoot what breaks.
- **Version and improve.** Version the team and propose changes with evidence and a way back.

Financial content is regulated, so nothing reaches a customer unless a person approved that exact version.

Every lab runs in the Claude desktop app in Code mode, in plain English. Learners type no code. All data is synthetic.

## Learning outcomes

- **LO1.** Conduct a compatibility assessment of Claude Cowork interfaces and use essential tools to integrate selected marketing components.
- **LO2.** Test the integrated marketing components and interfaces, identify integration errors and conduct basic troubleshooting.
- **LO3.** Propose changes to the integration plan from observed results, through versioning, and apply integration best practices.

## Topics covered

1. **Claude Cowork components, compatibility and integration** (K1–K3, A1–A2). The Claude suite, Claude Cowork and Claude Code, a brief history of AI, the harness, the agentic loop, and agentic AI vs AI agents. The systems behind the team, their components and interfaces, assessing compatibility, and the compatibility matrix. Agent instructions, skills and connectors with least privilege.
2. **Testing marketing integrations and troubleshooting errors** (K4–K6, A3–A4). Integration tools and techniques; every campaign as an integration test; protocols at each interface (MCP, OAuth, HTTPS APIs, hooks); the human approval gate; signs of integration errors.
3. **Versioning, change proposals and integration best practices** (K7, A5). Troubleshooting methodologies; always-on routines; responsible AI; versioning the team, change proposals and roll-back; agent teams with a QA loop.
